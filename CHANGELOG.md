## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-03

### Fixed

- **Dictionary definitions no longer show a `|` beside each bullet.**

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-02

### Added

- **One note type for every language Anki Miner mines, Japanese included.** Needs Anki 23.10 or later.
- **Lapis's card types:** word, word and sentence, click, sentence and audio.
- **Per-language extras on the back of the card.** Pinyin, jyutping, hanja, gender, plural, root and more, shown only when filled.
- **Gender colours on noun chips.**
- **Sentence translation under the sentence (`SentenceTranslation`).**
- **Right-to-left layout for Arabic, Persian and Hebrew.**
- **Regional fonts for Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters.**
- **Field names that Anki Miner maps on its own (Settings -> Cards & Anki -> Fill in automatically).**
- **Language tag (`Language` field).** Left empty, the card guesses the language from its text.
- **Example deck with one note per script group.**
